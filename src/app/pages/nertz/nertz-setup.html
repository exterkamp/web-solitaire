Nertz for two

Two players, one phone, laid down between you. Everybody plays at once, and the foundations in the middle are a race.

Setup

Sit at opposite ends of the phone. Each of you has a deck: thirteen cards in a Nertz pile with the top one face up, four work piles of one card each, and the other thirty-five in your stock. The eight foundations in the middle start empty.

Play

No turns: play as fast as you can. Aces open a foundation, and each foundation builds up in its own suit. Your work piles build down in alternating colours, and a run moves as a unit. Play from the top of your Nertz pile first. Turn your stock three at a time, and when it runs out, turn the waste back over. Both of you may play to the same foundation, and whoever gets there first lands it.

Scoring

When your Nertz pile is empty, call Nertz and the round ends. You score a point for every card you put on a foundation and lose two for every card left in your Nertz pile, so going out first is not the same as winning. If you are both stuck, flip through your hands, and then you either carry on or both agree to end the round. Rounds continue until somebody reaches the target.

Tap a card to send it somewhere sensible, or drag it to place it exactly. Both of you can drag at once.
